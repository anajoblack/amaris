Meet Dr. Amaris Perbi

About Dr. Nana Adjei Amaris Perbi

Dr. Nana Adjei Amaris Perbi is a Global Training Consultant and Executive Educator with
over 15 years of experience designing and delivering high-impact leadership, strategy, ESG,
risk, PMO, operational excellence, and digital transformation programs across multiple
continents. His work empowers executives, managers, and organizations to lead with
clarity, purpose, and measurable impact.

Professional Summary

Dr. Perbi is known for his scenario-driven, competency-based training methodology that transforms complex concepts into practical, actionable learning experiences. He has delivered over 70 professional development courses to leaders across the U.S., U.K., UAE, Europe, and Africa, strengthening leadership capability, improving organizational performance, and driving strategic transformation.

He holds a PhD in Business Administration, an MBA in Business Management, and a BBA in Management. His certifications include PMP, PgMP, RMP, Lean Six Sigma, CFA ESG Investing, CPT, CPTM, and CPTD.
